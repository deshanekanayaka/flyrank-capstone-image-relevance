## Images

The packshots come from Open Food Facts, and carry the CC BY-SA 4.0 license. The author
and the license for each image are in `manifest.csv`, one row per image. Five images are
committed, so the demonstration operates with no network. `scripts/fetch_corpus.py`
downloads the rest.
